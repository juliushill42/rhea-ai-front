## The Problem

Every AI tool on the market — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — sends your data to
someone else's server. Your queries, your documents, your codebase, your
client data. Gone. Logged. Trained on. Sold around.

For law firms, civil rights organizations, HBCUs, healthcare providers,
government contractors, and community-focused businesses — that's not
acceptable. And enterprise "private AI" solutions start at $50,000+.

---

## The Solution

RHEA runs on your hardware. Your network. Your domain.
No data leaves unless you say so.

- Talk to your AI from any device, anywhere in the world
- Your documents, your codebase, your memory — stays yours
- Automatic cloud backup if your local machine goes down
- Gets smarter over time — learns your organization's knowledge
